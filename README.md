# Joe Fernandez

I write about putting AI to work in banking and everyday jobs, and I build with it myself.

My career started in 1994, writing COBOL and PL/SQL against IBM DB2 on mainframes. Today I build and deploy machine learning models, and I write books about how organizations can use AI and stay in control of it. More at [thinkmorphic.com](https://thinkmorphic.com).

## Books

- **The Seatbelt Principle**: AI governance for banking executives who intend to move fast. [Kindle edition](https://a.co/d/04EPcVGE)
- **Climb It or Go Around It**: a free, role-by-role guide to using AI in the job you have (in progress). [thinkmorphic.com/climb](https://thinkmorphic.com/climb/)

## Selected projects

| Project | What it does | Result |
|---|---|---|
| [Used car valuation](https://github.com/FerndzJoe/Used-Car-Valuation) | Predicts a fair trade-in price from eleven attributes, comparing nine regression models | XGBoost, R² 0.958 |
| [Movie recommendations](https://github.com/FerndzJoe/RecommendationSystem-Movies) | Compares seven collaborative filtering algorithms on 1.1 million MovieLens ratings | NMF, RMSE 0.849 |
| [Diabetes risk](https://github.com/FerndzJoe/CDC-Diabetes-Health-Indicator) | Relates lifestyle factors to diabetes across 253,680 CDC survey records | Logistic regression, accuracy 0.87 |
| [Term deposit marketing](https://github.com/FerndzJoe/Comparing_Classifiers_Portugese_Bank) | Finds which bank customers are worth calling, across 41,188 campaign records | Logistic regression, ROC-AUC 0.93 |
